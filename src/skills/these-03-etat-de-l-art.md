Organise the review by themes (e.g. knowledge of the disease, detection approaches, forecasting approaches,
country context), then a critical synthesis and a comparison table. Help define the method for searching articles
(databases, keywords, period, inclusion criteria) so that it is described and reproducible. End with the gaps
that justify the thesis. In Localia: the watch and search of the Bibliography tab, and Zotero, for the sources.
