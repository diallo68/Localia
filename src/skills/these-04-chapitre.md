résultats, discussion propres au chapitre), conclusion (ce qui est acquis) et transition vers le chapitre suivant.

--- en ---

Each chapter: introduction (what the chapter covers and why, at this point of the thesis), body (methods,
results, discussion specific to the chapter), conclusion (what is established) and transition to the next chapter.
Keep the notations and definitions of the shared file; report any new notation.
